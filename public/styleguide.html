UNBOUND.
NO LIMITS.

A live identity system for college streetwear enthusiasts. Wear your own way. Our postmodernist approach uses bold typography, unconventional composition, and high contrast to celebrate individuality.

00 / Direction

BRAND-001 / Chosen direction

Audience

College streetwear enthusiasts, ages 18–24

Students seeking versatile style and personal expression. This is an audience hypothesis, not validated research.

Archetype

The Rebel

Independent, expressive, and confident without making unsupported product claims.

Visual direction

Postmodernist

Expressive type, asymmetry, and strong contrast reflect the hypothesis that youth fashion supports identity exploration.

06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Bold, direct, and authentic. Make room for individuality without misleading shoppers.

Brand headline

“WEAR YOUR OWN WAY.”

A confident invitation to express personal style.

Clear shopping language

“Choose a size before adding the shirt to your demo bag.” Direct guidance supports a real choice. No invented reviews, scarcity, or performance claims.
